## Design changes

The opening now says “Business IT. All connected.” and describes managed IT support, cyber security and telecoms for UK businesses. The folder remains intact. Establishment/UK coverage and three certification statements provide early, inspectable reassurance. Source panels stay on the landing page, with external source links opening separately only when chosen.

The people/process section uses an authentic support-engineer photograph from ITCS's current careers material, alongside the existing three clear steps. Customer tabs expose source-backed work context and longer, concrete passages. Secondary copy, navigation, form labels and Alison's conversation controls are larger and clearer. Small white CTA labels use a slightly deeper ITCS red. These improvements are not a claim of complete WCAG conformance.

Alison retains her interactive rig and British voice. Head texture resolution was increased to 2048px, normal-map intensity reduced, skin/jacket roughness separated and lighting/smile adjusted. The conversation has a larger share of the desktop layout. The GLB remains lazy-loaded and is approximately 3.6MB. The fallback is a fresh render of the same model. This improves the existing character; it does not turn the source model into a photographic human.
