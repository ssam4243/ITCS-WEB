# ITCS service printer experiment

The folder hero remains unchanged. The service-card grid below it is replaced by a printer inspired by the user's receipt-printer reference. Three core services sit to the left and three complementary services to the right. On small screens the choices move above the paper.

ITCS red print head, charcoal surroundings, existing paper grain and a serrated receipt edge connect it to the folder's physical stationery language. Selecting a service feeds a service brief out of the slot in one short animation; there is no looping motion, audio, scroll trapping or new dependency. Reduced motion and the site's motion preference show the selected brief immediately.

Receipts use the existing service data: benefit, four available areas of help, description, enquiry and detail actions. No fabricated pricing or time-limited offers. Links use the existing landing-page dialogs until individual pages are developed. Rapid selection cancels the previous feed; buttons remain available and keyboard operable. A polite status announces completion without reading the entire receipt on each change.

Validation: `test:printer` covers all six choices, rapid switching, contextual enquiry, keyboard activation, reduced motion, console errors and six viewport widths. The homepage regression retains folder, customer, navigation and layout checks.
